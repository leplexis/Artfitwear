What to include in your message

The more of this you can answer up front, the faster the quote.

Discipline & level

Figure skating or gymnastics, artistic or rhythmic, and the level or category the athlete competes in.

The date

The competition you're building toward. Costumes are booked by date, and season is busy — earlier is better.

The idea

Colors, the music, a sketch, or photos of costumes you like. A rough idea is plenty to start.

Stone coverage

Light, moderate or full. This moves the price more than anything else, so it's worth saying early.

Common questions

How far ahead should I order?

Six to eight weeks is comfortable for a competition dress or leotard, and longer for team orders. Rush work is sometimes possible — ask, and you'll get a straight answer.

Do you charge for a quote?

No. Quotes and the first fitting are free, and you'll have a firm price before any fabric is cut.

My skater is still growing. Is that a problem?

No. Costumes for growing athletes are built with extra seam allowance and adjustable straps where the design allows, and can be let out later.

Can you work from a photo of a costume I like?

Yes — as a starting point for colors, shape and stoning. Designs are adapted to the athlete rather than copied outright.

Do you fix costumes you didn't make?

Yes. Repairs, re-stoning and resizing are welcome whoever made the original.

What if the fit isn't right?

Try it on at collection. Minor adjustments after the fitting are included in the price.
